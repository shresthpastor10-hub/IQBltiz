StudyBlitz

StudyBlitz is a quick quiz game for students. Pick a subject, race StudyBot to the right answer and win trophies.

How to Play

  • Choose one subject: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics or Biology.
  • Choose Bot Mode and play against StudyBot.
  • A match has 5 questions. Each question has a 30 second timer.
  • You and StudyBot get one attempt per question.
  • Correct answer: +3 for you, -3 for your opponent (unless they already lost 3 on this question).
  • Wrong answer: -3 for you and you are frozen. Your opponent can still answer.
  • Both wrong: both get -3. Nobody answers in 30 seconds: both get 0.
  • One wrong and one silent: -3 and 0.
  • After 5 questions the higher score wins. Trophies change by 4 per point of difference (maximum 30).
  • The winner gains trophies, the loser loses the same amount, a draw changes nothing. Trophies can go below 0.
